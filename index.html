About Fundisha

Empowering Ugandan secondary school students through open, accessible, and syllabus-aligned digital learning resources.

Our Mission

To make quality education and learning resources more accessible for every secondary student across Uganda by eliminating geographic and financial barriers through free digital access.

Our Vision

To cultivate a long-term future where technology and curriculum-aligned study tools enable every Ugandan learner to explore subjects deeply, test their understanding, and achieve academic success.

What is Fundisha?

Fundisha is an education platform designed to make useful, accessible learning resources easier for students to discover and use. The platform curates revision notes, UNEB past papers, marking schemes, textbooks, and interactive quizzes organized systematically by class (Senior 1 to Senior 6) and subject.

Who Fundisha is For

The platform is designed primarily around students and learners in Ugandan secondary schools preparing for classroom tests, mid-term examinations, and national UNEB UCE and UACE exams. It additionally serves teachers, educators, and study groups seeking organized reference materials and structured practice questions.

Why Fundisha Exists

Secondary students frequently face fragmented, hard-to-find, or inaccessible study materials, outdated physical past papers, and limited access to textbooks. Fundisha addresses this challenge by organizing verified educational materials into an intuitive, lightweight, and mobile-friendly web experience available to all learners without charge.

Help Center & Guides

Learn how to make the most of Fundisha's digital curriculum library, past papers, quizzes, and AI tutor.

1. Finding Resources & UNEB Past Papers

Navigate to the Resources Library to filter by your secondary class (Senior 1 through Senior 6), specific subject, or material format (Notes, Past Papers, Textbooks, Practical Guides, Syllabus). Use the instant search bar to find exact topics or formula sheets.

2. Using the AI Study Tutor

The AI Tutor is tuned to provide step-by-step mathematical and scientific breakdowns, historical explanations, and Ugandan syllabus definitions. Type your question or click any of the high-yield suggestion prompts.

3. Bookmarking & Offline Access

Click the bookmark icon (📌) on any resource card to save it to your Saved Bookmarks. Your saved items are stored on your device and automatically synced to the cloud when you sign in.

4. NCDC & UNEB Guidelines Alignment

All curriculum topics conform to the National Curriculum Development Centre (NCDC) lower secondary competence-based framework and UNEB examination standards for UCE (O-Level) and UACE (A-Level).

Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2026 • Fundisha Uganda Secondary Initiative

1. Commitment to Student Privacy

Fundisha is built exclusively for education. We do not sell student data, track personal browsing habits, or serve commercial advertisements. Our goal is to provide a clean, safe, and focused digital learning environment.

2. Information We Collect

We collect minimal operational data necessary for your personalized study experience:

  • Study Profile: Your selected class level (e.g. Senior 4) and subjects for resource recommendations.
  • Study Metrics: Saved bookmarks, quiz attempt scores, and daily study streaks.
  • Account Credentials: If you choose to register, your email and password securely handled via Firebase Authentication.

3. Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored locally in your browser and synced to encrypted Google Cloud Firestore databases protected by strict security rules. Only authenticated users can access their own personal bookmarks and progress.

4. Your Rights & Data Deletion

You can clear your local cache, remove bookmarks, or reset your student profile at any time through the Settings page.

Terms of Service & Academic Guidelines

Last updated: January 2026 • Fundisha Learning Platform

1. Free Educational Access

Fundisha provides free access to past examination questions, revision notes, textbooks, and interactive study tools for personal study and classroom preparation. Resale or commercial exploitation of materials accessed through Fundisha is strictly prohibited.

2. Academic Integrity

All practice quizzes, revision guides, and past papers are provided to assist independent learning and revision. Learners are encouraged to test themselves honestly and review marking rationale for genuine mastery.

3. Curriculum Materials & Attribution

Past papers and examination formats reflect official Uganda National Examinations Board (UNEB) and National Curriculum Development Centre (NCDC) frameworks. Fundisha strives for accuracy across all solutions and marking guides.
